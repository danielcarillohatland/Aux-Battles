Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

AUX BATTLES is a free, non-commercial party game made as a personal portfolio project. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. This page explains what the game handles while you play.

What you give the game

  • A nickname you choose when you join a room.
  • The songs you pick (title and artist) and the short defense you may write.
  • Crowd votes, if the room votes instead of the AI judge.

The AI judge

In rooms judged by DJ AUX, each pick's title, artist and defense, together with the round's scenario, is sent to OpenRouter, which forwards it to an AI model provider to score the pick. Nicknames are not sent. Players agree to this when they join an AI room. The AI never hears audio. If you would rather not share your picks, ask the host to choose "The room" (crowd voting) instead.

How long it is kept

  • Room state (nicknames, picks, defenses, scores) is kept only while the room is live, so the game survives a server restart. A room is deleted after 4 hours, or 10 minutes after everyone has left.
  • Your phone or browser stores a session token so you can reconnect to the same room. It is removed when you start over and is useless once the room is gone.
  • Usage counts (for example "a room was created" or "a round finished") are stored with the room code only, never names or songs, to see whether the game works.
  • Server logs, kept by the hosting provider for a short time, may include IP addresses for security and abuse prevention.

Reports

If you report a player's name or defense from the "Help & privacy" panel, the reported text and your optional note are stored privately so the operator can review them.

Music

Song links open in services like Apple Music or YouTube, which have their own privacy policies. Song search may use a public music catalog; only the search text is sent.

Questions

Contact the developer through the portfolio site that linked you here. You can ask what is stored about a room or for a report to be deleted.
